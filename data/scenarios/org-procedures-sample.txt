SAMPLE COMPANY HANDBOOK (fictional, for testing the import)

Falls
If a resident falls, check for injury before moving them. Use the gait belt and two staff to help them up. The caregiver tells the manager the same shift. The manager calls the family within 24 hours and sends the incident form to the care manager within 3 days.

Emergencies
For any emergency, get the guardian's permission before calling 911. Then call the manager.

Missing resident
If a resident cannot be found, search the building and yard. Call the police immediately and then call the manager.

Suspected abuse
Staff who suspect abuse must tell the manager before reporting to anyone else. The manager investigates and reports to the state within 10 days if needed.

Call-ins
Staff who cannot work must call the manager at least 4 hours before the shift. The off-going caregiver stays until relieved.

Complaints
Family complaints are written on the complaint form and given to the administrator, who responds within 5 business days.
