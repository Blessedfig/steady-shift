Procedure double-check

Ask in everyday words. Get your home's answer in plain steps.

No digging through a binder, and no feeling silly for asking. Questions can be asked without a resident's name.

Calm incident reports

From a tired shift note to a report anyone can read.

Staff write it the way they'd tell a coworker. The app turns it into facts, times and actions taken, and the caregiver checks it before it goes anywhere.

  • Takes out blame and guesses about motive, keeps what prevents the next fall
  • Shows who needs to be told and the deadline for each
  • Progress notes too: your own words, plus a one-tap professional version
Overnight triage

Managers sleep. Night staff are never left alone with a hard call.

Every after-hours question gets sorted: call 911, wake the on-call manager, send a quiet heads-up, or hold for the morning brief.

  • Built from your own procedures and on-call list
  • "Not sure" about safety always means call. Backup rings if no answer
  • The emergency button is on every staff screen, one tap away

Questions

What owners usually ask us.

Is Steady Shift HIPAA compliant?

Not yet, and we won't say it is until it's true. Right now the app runs on made-up sample residents only. Before any home enters real resident information, we'll have secure servers, a written HIPAA program and signed business associate agreements in place. The app is being designed around privacy from the first screen so that step is ready when it comes.

What happens after the free trial?

You get 14 days with every screen unlocked. Near the end, the app shows the plans so you can pick one. If you don't, the trial simply ends. No card is asked for up front, so nothing is charged by surprise.

Do caregivers have to pay?

No. The home pays one price, and every caregiver at that home uses it free. A nurse you work with can join free too.

Do we need new devices?

No. It runs in a web browser on phones, tablets and computers, and there's an Android app. Small homes can run the whole shift from one shared tablet with a personal PIN for each person. See how the one-tablet setup works.

Does it replace our policies or our nurse?

No. Your own policies sit on top of the law, and the app checks they never fall below code. It supports staff judgment and your nurse's delegation. It is not medical or legal advice, and it never replaces a call to 911.

How do you keep the rule answers accurate?

Every answer shows where it comes from and when we last checked it, plus how sure we are: "Rule says", "Best reading" or "Open question". Anything not checked in 12 months goes back into review. When rules truly conflict, we help you decide and document instead of guessing.

We're thinking about opening a home. Can this help?

Yes. Our free guide walks through which license fits, the DQA application, required training, contracts and the citations that catch new homes. Read the free guide.
