A calmer home

The murky middle

For the situations nobody's handbook covers, and the gap between the floor and the office.

Most hard days aren't a clear emergency or a clear rule. A resident pushes back, two caregivers remember a shift differently, a new federal rule seems to clash with the binder, or the owner wants answers the manager doesn't have time to write up.

Those moments are where fights start, citations happen and good staff quit. This app gives everyone the same calm starting point: what the rule says, what your home decided, and the words to say it without blaming anyone.

Small and medium homes know their residents as people, not numbers. The rules still matter, but staff shouldn't feel frozen by them. The app handles the system side quietly so caregivers can stay focused on the person in front of them.

Up

Caregivers raise concerns in writing without going around their manager. Patterns reach the owner without naming who asked.

Down

Owners and managers send policy changes as short cards with the reason attached, and see who has confirmed them.

Across

Shift-to-shift handoffs stay factual, so the next shift starts with information instead of tension.

What it does

Six tools staff reach for during a shift.

Procedure double-check

Ask a question in everyday words and get the answer from your home's procedure, with the rule behind it.

  • DHS 88 and DHS 83 first
  • Shows which level each rule comes from
  • Flags house rules that fall short of code

Incident reports that stay calm

Staff describe what happened the way they'd tell a coworker. The app turns it into facts, times and actions taken.

  • Fills the fields your MCO and the state ask for
  • Removes blame and guesses about motive
  • Staff review and sign before it is sent

Overnight triage

Every after-hours question gets sorted: call 911, wake the on-call manager, send a quiet heads-up, or hold for morning.

  • Backup on-call if no answer
  • “Not sure” about safety always means call
  • 6 AM summary for the manager

Question tracking

See what staff keep asking about, so training goes where it's needed instead of where someone made a mistake.

  • Patterns by topic and shift
  • Monthly roll-up for the owner
  • Shows gaps, not people to blame

Your house policies, kept private

Each home's own rules sit on top of the law. They can be stricter than code, never looser, and no other home sees them.

  • Rule changes arrive as short “what changed for you” cards
  • Manager approves before staff see them
  • Staff confirm in under two minutes

Resident behavior support In design

Step-by-step guidance for each resident's behavior plan, plus short trainings on respectful ways to redirect and encourage, so every shift responds the same way.

  • Follows the resident's own plan
  • Respectful-influence training for staff
  • Tracks what worked and what didn't

Rule says

Can I set up meds in a cup for later?

In an adult family home, no. Meds stay in their original container until you give them.

  1. Leave meds in the pharmacy packaging.
  2. Take them out one resident at a time, at the time they're due.
  3. Sign the record after each one.
DHS 88.07(3)(a) · checked Nov 2026

Not legal advice. Check with your licensing specialist or attorney for your situation.

Our promises

What we will never do.

No games with your staff

No streaks, badges, points or rankings. Training shows what's left, not a score.

No blame built in

Reports describe what happened, never who is at fault. Questions show up as patterns, not names.

No hidden emergency

The emergency button is on every staff screen. Nobody needs permission to call 911.

No tricks at checkout

No pre-checked add-ons, no guilt-trip wording and no hidden cancel button.
