Accessibility

Built to be easy to use, even at 3 AM

Last updated November 26, 2026.

Our goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA across the website and the app. Caregivers use Steady Shift tired, in a hurry and on small phones, so we design for that first.

What we do

  • Large text (17px body) and large tap targets, so buttons are easy to hit.
  • Color contrast that meets WCAG AA, and color is never the only way something is shown.
  • A "Skip to main content" link, clear headings and labeled sections for screen readers and keyboards.
  • Calmer motion when your device asks for reduced motion, and a dark night mode.
  • Plain-language wording, checked against our own design guide.
  • An automatic accessibility check runs on every change to the website.

Known gaps

  • Photos on the home page are still being chosen.
  • The test app has not yet been checked by people who use screen readers every day. We plan to do that before launch.

Tell us about a barrier

If anything is hard to use, we want to know. Email us at owner@theblessedfigtree.com, or use "Ideas and fixes" inside the test app.
