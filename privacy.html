Privacy

Your privacy, in plain words

Last updated November 28, 2026. Steady Shift is in private testing and does not hold real resident health information yet.

This is a plain-language draft. It will be reviewed by an attorney, and updated, before Steady Shift is used with real residents.

The public website

  • We do not use advertising, analytics, tracking pixels or cookies on this website.
  • The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub keeps standard server logs, such as your IP address, as described in the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Our fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads. See the Google privacy policy.

The private test app and Owner Desk

  • Only invited people, or people with a free trial code, can sign in. To let you in, we store your email address, your sign-in history and a password you choose (stored only in a protected, scrambled form) with our sign-in and database provider, Supabase.
  • Ideas, feedback and notes you send are stored there too, and the small project team can read them.
  • If you start a free trial, we also store the trial code, the name you give and the kind of home you run, so we can set up your trial and follow up.
  • Sample records you type into the test app stay in your own browser's storage on that device, not on our servers. "Reset sample data" on the home screen clears them.
  • The "Speak" button on care notes uses your browser's own speech service. In Chrome, Google turns the speech into text.
  • You are signed out automatically after 12 hours without use.
  • Sign-in codes and account emails are sent through our email provider, Zoho Mail, which receives your email address to deliver them.
  • If an AI helper is turned on (for example "Do it by chat", the ideas chat, or reading a procedure you upload), the text you send it is processed by Anthropic, the maker of the Claude AI model, to write the answer. AI answers can be wrong, so check anything important against your home's procedures and the rule. Do not include names or health details.
  • If you choose a paid plan online, payment is handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number. Stripe shares your email, plan and payment status with us so the app stays open for you.

Please do not enter real resident information

Everything in the test app is made up. Do not type real resident names, health details, or anything else that could identify a resident. Before Steady Shift holds protected health information, we will put HIPAA safeguards and signed business associate agreements in place and update this page.

What we don't do

We do not sell your information or share it for advertising. We share it only with the service providers named on this page, to run Steady Shift, or when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your account and anything you have sent us. Email us at owner@theblessedfigtree.com.

Who this is for

Steady Shift is made for adults who work in or run care homes, home care agencies and family caregiving. It is not meant for children.
