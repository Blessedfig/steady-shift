App screenshots used on the home page (`app-*.webp`). They are taken from `demo.html`, which shows the app's
screens with made-up sample residents only (Maple Grove AFH, Maya, Dee, Jo, Glen). Never use a screenshot with
real resident or staff information. To refresh them, open `demo.html#card`, `#report`, `#triageResult`, `#owner`
or `#home`, screenshot the phone at 2x, and save as WebP about 600px wide.

`care-together.webp`, `night-shift.webp` and `handoff.webp` are AI-generated illustrations (made-up people, no real residents), made in ChatGPT for this
site in the Sky and Sun palette, then resized to 1200x800 WebP. Keep new pictures in the same style.

`app/art/*.webp` are 720x480 copies of the same three pictures for the app's calm screens only (sign-in, academy and
staff training, family updates, clock-out). Keep them off work screens (meds, reports, checklists).
