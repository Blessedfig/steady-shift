Why one shared tablet works for small homes

💸

No phones to buy or manage

One basic tablet for the house. Staff don't have to put work apps on their own phones, and nothing walks out the door when someone leaves.

👆

Simple enough for day one

Big buttons, plain words, and only what the shift needs: checklist, medications, care notes, reports, meals, cleaning, and who to call.

🧾

Every entry has a name

Each person unlocks with their own PIN, so notes, doses and reports are signed by the person who did the work. Fewer "who did this?" conversations.

Setup in about 15 minutes

The app walks you through it, one step at a time.

  1. Your home. Pick small adult family home, family care at home, or a bigger home. The app chooses sensible settings for you.
  2. People and PINs. Add the people who will use it. Each one picks a private PIN.
  3. Privacy checklist. Eight quick checks for the tablet itself: passcode, a private spot, updates, Find My, and more, with the steps for iPad or Android.
  4. Lock it to the app. Turn on Guided Access (iPad) or App pinning (Android), so the tablet only runs Steady Shift.
  5. Finish. Name the tablet, print a one-page tablet card for the wall, and you're running.

Training is built in

"Learn the tablet" is seven short lessons, about 15 minutes in all, right on the tablet. Each one has a picture, a practice step and a quick question.

  • Unlock with your name and PIN, with a practice keypad
  • The big buttons, and what red means
  • Medications and the dose alarm
  • Care notes in your own words, made professional with one tap
  • Reporting, and who to call at 2 AM
  • The shift board and hand-off note
  • Locking it and keeping it private

The person in charge sees who has finished, and gets two extra lessons on PINs and lost tablets.

Privacy built in

HIPAA allows shared devices when each person has their own sign-in, the screen locks itself, and there is a record of who looked at what. The tablet does all three.

  • A personal PIN for each person, with lockouts after wrong tries
  • Locks after each person, and after a few minutes without a tap
  • Logs every unlock and every screen opened, by name
  • Never shows pay, owner reports or HR files
  • Medicine alarms never show medicine names on the lock screen
  • A lost tablet can be removed from the app

We're in a no-real-records test phase. Real resident information starts once our secure servers and signed business associate agreements are in place.
